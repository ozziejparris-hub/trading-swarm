## 🚨 Flags Requiring Attention

### [CRITICAL] geo_elo confirmed unfit — STR-003 signal architecture broken at the root

**This is the most significant finding since the project began.** `geo_elo` (and
`geo_elo_active`, derived from it) has been confirmed to have five structural defects,
confirmed empirically on August 15:

1. **Sign error** in `_compute_geo_elo`: uses `expected = 1.0 − price`, but `price`
   already equals P(traded outcome wins) for BOTH sides — empirically confirmed by pairing
   Yes/No trades within 60s on the same market and finding they sum to 1.000 ± 0.001. No
   trades are produced on the No side.
2. **Improper as a scoring rule**: a zero-skill trader buying favoured No positions earns
   free expected rating gain (2·price − 1 > 0).
3. **Sell contamination**: 35.7% of qualifying trades folded under an inverted
   win-condition with no side filter.
4. **Double-counting**: 52.3% of (trader, market) pairs have multiple qualifying trades,
   counting decision fragments not decisions.
5. **Uncalibrated parameters**: K-schedule, tier thresholds, decay half-life — none have a
   calibration record. The 2175/1800 thresholds were copied from the similarly
   uncalibrated `comprehensive_elo` system.

**Consequence for STR-003:** every signal registered to date was generated using a broken
instrument. The LEGENDARY pool (geo_elo_active >= 2175) may have selected substantially
for favourite-betting No traders rather than genuinely skilled forecasters. Registering
new STR-003 signals on this basis would compound an already-suspect signal history.

**What exists now:** a replacement scoring rule (proper Brier-based, correct price
normalisation, calibration gate passed, cap5 weighting, empirical-Bayes shrinkage) was
built and validated on August 15. Out-of-sample result is null, directionally positive,
underpowered. The new metric's LEGENDARY tier has only 15/81 overlap with the current
geo_elo LEGENDARY tier — the populations are mostly different.

**Recommended action:** Pause new STR-003 signal generation until Oscar decides whether
to (a) cut over to the replacement metric and reconstitute the pool, or (b) define a
transitional path. This is a scope decision requiring Oscar's direction, not a
maintenance fix. Document in `brain/decisions/`.

### [HIGH] Phase 2 live recording still not started — n loss is permanent and accelerating

Phase 2 (live paper trading) remains the primary experiment as of 2026-08-09 but the

**Recommended action:** Build B7 (`paper_trades` table schema + scorer) in the next
