## 📈 Brier Score Detail

### ⚠ Methodology caveat — all numbers this week are affected by two compounding factors

1. **geo_elo is broken:** Pool C membership and geo_elo_active weighting are computed
   on a confirmed-unfit metric. These Brier numbers describe observational Pool C
   behavior under the broken instrument, not calibrated accuracy.

2. **Discovery-gap closure caused a large n jump:** The sweep resolved ~59,236 markets
   in the DB this week. Markets that actually resolved in the Jul 25 – Aug 24 window but
   weren't previously in the DB are now included. The 7d GEO sample went from n=2 to n=84;
   30d GEO from n=16 to n=99. Week-on-week Brier changes partly reflect sample composition
   changes, not calibration changes.

### Geopolitics (7d, n=84)

**Brier=0.0895**, naive=0.2595, edge=**+0.170**, DirAcc=**59.5%**

Strongest 7d GEO reading in system history. Pool C geo traders were correctly directional
on roughly 3 in 5 contested geo markets that resolved this week. The edge vs naive (+0.170)
is substantial. With n=84 this is the first 7d geo reading that carries meaningful weight.

Caveat: the n=84 sample is new to the DB this week — these markets actually resolved over
the past 7 days but many weren't captured until the sweep ran. The result is valid (correct
resolution dates from Gamma) but should not be compared directly to last week's n=2 reading.

### Geopolitics (30d, n=99)

**Brier=0.3149**, naive=0.2580, edge=**-0.057** ⚠, DirAcc=**56.6%**

The 30d geo window is slightly worse than naive on Brier, but directionally positive at
56.6%. This apparent contradiction is explained by calibration — Pool C traders are right
directionally more than half the time, but bet with near-certainty confidence on a minority
of markets where they are wrong, producing high Brier penalty. This is YIELD_HARVESTER
contamination: a few Pool C traders betting 0.92+ probability on markets that resolved
the other way inflate the average materially.

The improvement from 0.4532 (Aug 17, n=16) to 0.3149 (n=99) reflects both genuine sample
dilution (the worst-performing Aug 17 markets now have more context) and the inclusion of
higher-quality markets from the sweep. The negative Brier edge at n=99 is the cleaner signal.

### Elections (7d, n=51)

**Brier=0.3623**, naive=0.3381, edge=**-0.024** ⚠, DirAcc=**49.0%**

Pool C geo traders are essentially random on Elections markets over a 7-day window.
DirAcc=49.0% is below-random (though not meaningfully so at n=51). The negative Brier
edge (-0.024) is small but in the wrong direction.

### Elections (30d, n=78)

**Brier=0.3506**, naive=0.3133, edge=**-0.037** ⚠, DirAcc=**50.0%**

Random. DirAcc exactly at baseline. Brier worse than naive. This is consistent with the
all-2026 baseline finding (n=1,111, DirAcc=54%, Brier worse than naive) — Pool C geo
traders have no demonstrated edge in Elections markets. The 65.6% DirAcc reading from
last week (n=32) was a small-sample artefact.
