## PART 1 — Premise verification

**1. What `point_gap` denotes** [V, `scripts/trader_skill_metric_v2d.py:189-190`]:
```python
point_denom = base_w.sum()
point_gap = float((base_w * p_won).sum() / point_denom - (base_w * p_price).sum() / point_denom)
```
`point_gap` is `weighted_mean(won) − weighted_mean(price)` — **each
population's own mean edge**, computed independently per call. It is
**not** a differenced quantity between cohort and placebo. **The prompt's
premise is correct.** Cohort-minus-placebo must be computed as the
difference of two separately-computed `point_gap` values, which is exactly
what was missing from the decay pre-registration's §4 gate (it inherits
Track 2's Gate A, which tests the cohort's own `point_gap` and CI, never
a gap).

**2. Same construction for both** [V, `scripts/trader_skill_metric_v2f.py:419-420`]:
`measure_oos` is called identically for cohort and placebo — same
function, same `T_SPLIT`, same `cap5` weighting (hardcoded inside
`measure_oos`), same `seed=SEED`, same `reps=GATE_REPS_LOCAL`. The only
difference between the two calls is which trader-address list is passed.
The differing `n` (3,795 cohort vs. 2,693 placebo positions) is expected —
matching is on pre-split activity profile, not on OOS position count, and
post-split trading volume naturally differs between matched groups. **Not
a sign of different construction.**

**3. The 08-15 figures, re-verified directly from `metric_v2f_oos_result`**

## PART 3 — Diagnosing the movement

**6. Decomposing the swing.** Cohort fell `0.031598 → 0.020787`
(Δ = **−0.010812**, a 34.2% relative decline). Placebo rose
`0.012707 → 0.027952` (Δ = **+0.015246**, a 120.1% relative increase — more
than doubling). The total gap swing is `+0.018892 → −0.007166`
(Δ = **−0.026058**). **The placebo's rise accounts for 58.5% of the total
swing; the cohort's fall accounts for 41.5%.** The placebo moved more, in
absolute terms, than the cohort did.

**Correcting the task's framing of the 2026-09-04 lineage prediction** —
checked directly against the doc, not assumed: `2026-09-04-thesis-population-lineage.md`
does **not** predict the *overall* gap would trend negative on both sides.
Its prediction is narrower and was about a specific, named subset: the 242
newly-discoverable markets from the (now-closed) discovery-gap sweep.
Quoted directly: on that 242-market subset alone, cohort's edge is
`−0.0410` (n=161) and placebo's is `−0.0316` (n=102) — **both negative, as
predicted, and this specific prediction held**: "the cohort-vs-placebo gap
moves by about −0.0003 when the 242-set is included vs. excluded... a
negligible shift relative to either figure's own uncertainty." **That
doc's own text separately, explicitly noted the overall inversion as an
aside and declined to investigate it**: "today's live re-run shows
placebo's point estimate *exceeding* cohort's, +0.032 vs +0.020 — itself a
consequence of the already-documented, unrelated DB-drift
non-reproducibility, not of the 242-set; flagged for completeness, not
chased further here." **So there is no prediction to record as wrong** —
the 242-set prediction was correct and about a different, much smaller
mechanism than what actually moved the overall numbers. The task's own
framing conflated the two; the lineage doc kept them separate throughout.
(Note: that doc's `+0.032`/`+0.020` figures differ slightly from this
session's `0.0280`/`0.0208` — plausibly because that doc's "today's live
re-run" used `metric_v2f_intersection_cohort`, the 295-trader *proxy*
population Track 2 itself found was not the true cohort, rather than the
true 169-trader cohort reconstructed for the first time in Track 2's own
diagnostic [I, not independently confirmed] — the qualitative inversion
appears robust across at least two different cohort constructions, though
this is not chased further here.)

**7. What drove each side's movement — attributed at row level, not
assumed.** Both sides' population growth is **100% attributable to the
identical mechanism** [V, live query, same construction Track 2 already
used for the cohort, now also run for the placebo]:

```
COHORT:        new_entries=0  preexisting_now_resolved=3795  (100.00%)
PLACEBO/control: new_entries=0  preexisting_now_resolved=2693  (100.00%)
```

Every single OOS position on both sides existed (by `entry_timestamp`) as
of the original result's `generated_at` (2026-08-15T19:36:56) and has
since transitioned from pending to resolved. **Zero genuinely new entries
on either side.** This rules out "one side got new positions the other
didn't" as an explanation for the divergence — the mechanism is identical
on both arms. The divergence must therefore come from the *outcomes*
(won/lost, at entry price) of the specific positions that happened to
resolve during this three-week window, not from an asymmetry in which
positions were even eligible to move.

A concentration check on the placebo side [V]: the top 5 of 118 placebo
traders by mean edge account for **31.5%** of the total summed edge —
meaningfully concentrated, but not so extreme as to look like a single
outlier or data artifact (the largest single market by position count,
45 positions, actually carries a *negative* mean edge of −0.096 — the rise
is not simply "one lucky market"). **Suggestive of a broad-based shift with
some concentration, not conclusive of either a clean population effect or
a clean artifact** — this is as far as this bounded check can adjudicate
without the additional work of checking whether the newly-resolved subset
is representative of each group's full population.

**8. Is the placebo construction still sound?** `match_control` matches on
pre-split position count, market breadth, and activity span — **never on
edge, and never on anything about post-split (OOS) behavior**. Nothing
about matching quality on those pre-split variables should mechanically
predict differential *post-split resolution luck* in either direction.
Given that both sides' growth mechanism is identical (§7) and the matching
logic itself was fixed before either side's OOS outcome was knowable, **the
more parsimonious explanation, on the evidence available here, is
regression toward the mean in the cohort — not drift in the matching**:
the cohort was explicitly *selected* on presplit outperformance
(`significance-95 + M≥10 + edge≥0.02`), which is exactly the condition
under which some of that presplit edge is expected to be partly noise that
mean-reverts out-of-sample. The placebo, selected on activity profile
alone and never on edge, has no analogous selection pressure — its rise is
consistent with ordinary, unbiased OOS sampling variance landing on the
positive side this time, not with the matching mechanism having broken.
**This is a reasoned judgment [I], not a proven one** — distinguishing it
cleanly from "the matched variables fail to control for some other
confound" would require checking whether the specific markets/positions
that resolved during this window are representative of each group's
broader population, which this bounded check does not do.

## PART 4 — What this means

**9. Should the decay measurement proceed as pre-registered?** **No, not
as currently written.** The concern in the prompt is confirmed: the decay
pre-registration's §4 gate, inherited verbatim from Track 2's Gate A, tests
only the **cohort's own** point estimate, CI bounds, sign, and population
growth — every one of those checks would **pass** under this session's
numbers (cohort point_gap positive, within Track 2's own CI bounds
trivially since it is the identical reproduction, population growing,
100% attributed) — **while the actual quantity the entire decay
measurement exists to track (the gap) has moved to a negative point
estimate with a wide, zero-including CI.** A gate that only checks the
cohort side cannot detect this. **What needs amending, not amended here**:
§4 needs its own explicit criterion computed on the **paired gap
distribution** (exactly as done in Part 2 above — this check's own method
is the natural candidate for that amendment), not the cohort alone.
Whether that criterion should require the gap's point estimate to stay
positive, or merely require its CI to still be consistent with the
original `+0.0189`, or something else, is a decision for whoever amends
that pre-registration, not decided here.

**Should it not proceed at all?** Not that either, on this evidence alone
— the gap's CI does not exclude zero, so a real, if diminished, gap cannot
be ruled out either. The honest state is: **proceed only after §4 is
amended to gate on the paired gap, computed the way this document computed
it** — running the twelve-point ladder against a gate that cannot see the
one thing that matters would risk exactly the outcome the original task
worried about: passing a gate while measuring something that may no
longer be there.

**10. Implication for the canonical skill metric design's §1(b) "source of
profit" question.** If this negative-point-estimate, wide-CI result holds
up under further scrutiny, **§1(b) may currently have nothing established
to attribute** — the question "does profit come from direction or
execution" presupposes a demonstrated cohort-over-placebo profit to
decompose, and that demonstration is exactly what has stopped holding at
N=0. This does **not**, however, moot §1(a) (directional skill via the
Gómez-Cram randomized-direction benchmark) — that test is a **within-cohort**
comparison (actual PnL vs. a randomized-direction null on the same
trader's own trades) and does not depend on the placebo comparison at all;
it could still find genuine directional skill even if the cohort-vs-placebo
gap has narrowed or inverted. **This finding raises the priority of running
§1(a) directly**, since it is now the more load-bearing of the two
remaining live questions in the design, and does not inherit this
document's uncertainty about the gap.

This result is also, informally, very close to — but not identical to —
formally executing `2026-08-21-discovery-gap-closure-prereg.md` §E's
still-unrun "second measurement" (which specifies its own `_corrected`
table-naming and delta-CI protocol on a freshly-run
`build_presplit_cohort`/`match_control`, not Track 2's already-frozen
lists). This check reuses Track 2's frozen population rather than
re-deriving it, per this task's own constraint, so it should be read as a
strong preview of what §E's formal measurement would likely show, not as
having formally executed it.
