by a **separate, unconditional, direct `UPDATE`** in the exact function
step 3 touches — `fast_resolution_check.py`'s `batch_update_resolved_markets`,

mmr_result = mark_market_resolved(
    conn, market_id, winning_outcome=winner,

# last_checked is not a canonical-path column -- stays a direct write, unchanged.

`last_checked` is always `datetime.now()`, on every write, **completely

argument on one call, three lines above a write it does not touch. A
market resolved 200 days ago, discovered today, gets `last_checked =
today` either way. `requeue_resolved_market_traders.py` sees it as newly
resolved either way.
